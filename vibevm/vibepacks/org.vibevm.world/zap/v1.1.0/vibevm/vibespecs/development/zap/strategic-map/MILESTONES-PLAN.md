## Accepted interface decisions

Canonical milestone records use independent milestone, revision and achievement
identities. Existing outcome, obligation, work and evidence subjects provide the
normal relevant-basis/economic scope; exact new-record revision and fingerprints
are additionally validated in both simulation and application. No new generic
subject variant is needed solely to name a map object. Acceptance meaning and
proof applicability are distinct from a record's presentation or contribution
revision; historical achievement is retained through all transformations.

Information discovery creates a data proposal. A selection binds an exact
opportunity and applicable decision/source basis to one candidate ordinary Work
identity before lowering. It creates no Work itself. Compatible benefit/cost
dimensions are compared honestly; unknown and overlapping estimates remain
unknown. The planning hook validates selection bindings and reuse.

An immutable milestone-plan proposal covers the required current outcome and
strategy obligations, names its focus/frontier and explicitly retains distant
horizons. A privileged adopted pointer keyed by outcome distinguishes commitment
from discovery. An immutable refinement record keyed by LoweringId binds the
exact graph, adopted-plan state and every new work rationale, selected research,
retained-work/evidence comparison and retry lineage. These are additive records;
the existing LoweringApplied and LoweredWorkBinding wire shapes remain intact.

The mandatory policy check runs inside apply_lowering_kernel before writes, so
effect simulation and application share it. Once an outcome has an adopted
milestone plan, a different strategy must update the adopted plan before it can
lower work. A title-based duplicate detector or an optional agent instruction
does not satisfy this policy. Semantic comparison rationales remain reviewable
claims; the kernel checks their bindings and conservation, not arbitrary prose.

Initial milestone creation and first plan adoption use a narrow validated
initial-baseline rule. It binds the exact candidate strategy and active
charter/intent/outcome, requires no adopted milestone plan and no lowering, and
cannot mistake imported active/accepted/unresolved work for a fresh baseline.
Once any plan is adopted or execution has been lowered, dynamic additions use
semantic economics admission, even before the first external launch. The rule
uses real outcome/obligation/current-work subjects; it does not invent a Work
target solely to reuse the older initial-lowering exemption. Revisions and
transformations remain semantic, and achievement remains witnessed proof.

## Dynamic reassessment

The Owner asked whether milestones can be created dynamically during reassessment;
the implementation commitment includes that behavior.
A reassessment may introduce a new milestone, split or merge boundaries, change
contributions or choose another route. The successor adopted plan must preserve
all required obligations and useful retained work/evidence or account for their
valid dispositions. Creation of a milestone alone creates no executable task.
The implementation must test addition of a new boundary during reassessment;
a fixed initial milestone catalog is insufficient.
